# Campaign - The Ember Crown

**Premise.** The old crown of the city of [[Emberfall]] was lost in a fire. Rival houses are searching for it, and the party is hired to find it first.

## Hooks

- A merchant pays the party to escort a cart over the ashen road.
- A letter with a burnt seal arrives at the inn.
- A sealed gate under the city has been opened from inside.

## Factions

- The Ash Court wants the crown returned to the throne.
- The Lantern Guild wants the crown melted down.
